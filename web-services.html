How Website Pricing Works
SEC Freelance separates website work into updates, development and custom projects. Updates are fixed-price tickets for existing SEC-supported websites and are designed for predictable changes. Development services cover new pages, website foundations and individual website enhancements. Custom projects are quoted separately when a request involves a major redesign, substantial restructuring, custom functionality, complex integrations, or work on a website outside the SEC-supported website system.

Website Update Tickets

Website update tickets are available for websites previously designed, developed, or maintained by SEC Freelance. Tickets are intended for predictable, fixed-scope updates within an established website structure. Websites built by third parties, major redesigns, substantial structural changes, custom functionality, or complex integrations may require a separate project quotation.

Light Update Minor changes to existing website content, including text, images, links, contact information, or small adjustments within existing sections. Small, predictable changes that don't alter the site's underlying structure or design system.
9,360
Standard Update Moderate updates to an existing website, such as adding a section, creating a standard content block, adding a blog post, or making several related content changes. More involved than a Light Update, but still within the site's existing design and technical structure.
14,040
